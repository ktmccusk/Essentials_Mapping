## YOUR TASK

Your task is to review the attached course syllabus and complete the competency alignment table by mapping each learning outcome to its corresponding AACN Essentials (2026) Level 2 sub-competencies and NONPF Nurse Practitioner Role Competencies (2022/2024).

## REFERENCE FRAMEWORKS

You must draw exclusively from these two sources:

**1. AACN Essentials: Core Competencies for Professional Nursing Education (2021)**
- Use **Level 2 (Advanced-Level Nursing Education)** sub-competencies only
- Sub-competencies are coded by Domain number, Competency number, and letter — e.g., **2.3j**, **4.5i**, **9.2a**
- There are 10 Domains:
  1. Knowledge for Nursing Practice
  2. Person-Centered Care
  3. Population Health
  4. Scholarship for the Nursing Discipline
  5. Quality and Safety
  6. Interprofessional Partnerships
  7. Systems-Based Practice
  8. Informatics and Healthcare Technologies
  9. Professionalism
  10. Personal, Professional, and Leadership Development

**2. NONPF Nurse Practitioner Role Competencies (2022; title updated November 2024)**
- These competencies build on and extend the AACN Level 2 sub-competencies with NP role-specific behaviors
- Domains mirror the 10 AACN domains with NP-specific language and additions
- Use the exact competency codes as they appear in the NONPF document

## INSTRUCTIONS

1. **Read the full syllabus**, paying close attention to the course description and each numbered learning outcome.

2. **Locate the competency alignment table** described above.

3. **For each learning outcome**, identify all AACN Level 2 sub-competencies and NONPF NP Role Competencies that are meaningfully aligned — meaning the learning outcome, if achieved, would constitute direct evidence of attainment of that sub-competency. 4. **Replace the `***` placeholder** on the `AACN Essentials:` line with the appropriate sub-competency codes, formatted as a comma-separated list:
   > **AACN Essentials:** 2.3j, 4.5i, 9.2a

## PROGRAM CONTEXT

This is a graduate-level, clinically intensive course in the AG-ACNP/AG-CNS program. Learning outcomes are written at the **entry-level competent** performance level for advanced practice in acute care settings, consistent with Level 2 of the AACN Essentials. The clinical context is adult and older adult patients across acute, critical, and complex chronic care settings. Both AG-ACNP and AG-CNS roles are served by this curriculum, though NONPF competencies specifically reflect the NP role.

When mapping, interpret learning outcomes through the lens of:
- Advanced clinical decision-making and diagnostic reasoning in acute care
- Person-centered and evidence-based care planning
- Interprofessional collaboration in complex inpatient and transitional care environments
- Competency-based behavioral performance expectations, not content coverage
